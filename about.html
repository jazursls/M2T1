About Me

Hi there! I'm Reivin "Jaz" Rosales, a passionate multimedia artist and filmmaker.
I'm currently studying at Far Eastern University Institute of Technology
under the Bachelor of Multimedia Arts program.

I have been into multimedia arts and filmmaking since I was in Junior Highschool.
Being one of the students who pioneered and started my highschool's multimedia club
which is still active to this day. I have a deep passion in telling stories and creaitng
art in many forms.

I began my journey in multimedia arts and filmmaking during the pandemic.
This is when I began to learn video editing. Soon, me and my friend would
being to write scripts for our short film ideas. It wasn't until during our
first year in college until we began to work on actually making the film
since we now had the time, budget, and skills. Which is the birth of our first
short film, Gemma Marie.

With this, I dream to be able to do more short films and tell more stories.
With the hopes that one day, I am able to direct a full length feature film.
